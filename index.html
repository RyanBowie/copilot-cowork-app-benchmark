What this does not establish

Task consumption is not a universal model ranking.

  • Two repetitions per configuration are not a reliable variance estimate or a promised price. Order, caching, service load and internal builder routing remain possible influences.
  • Meeting the 85-point and critical-check gate is not full coverage or production readiness. Connection-error/no-fallback fault testing remains unverified (BC09). Large-list pagination, real user authorization, concurrency and scale are not established by the small synthetic fixture.
  • The selected Cowork model is not proof that every internal app-building worker used that model.
  • Test 1's preview-delivery/cache problems affected its Opus results. They cannot be attributed solely to intrinsic model quality.
  • A shared connection and a synthetic persona switch are not production authorization. Historical sequential oversell checks do not prove concurrent safety or atomic updates across two tables.
  • Test 1's active-generation ceiling was not instrumented reliably; total wall time includes browser recovery and adapter calibration and is not a model-speed measure.
  • Test 1, the archived Dataverse attempt and the completed SharePoint Test 2 differ in backend availability and method. Do not call their cost differences the isolated price of a connector. The retired wrong-tenant SharePoint preflight is separate setup, not a measured model failure.
  • Credits here are observed task consumption, not a currency amount or tenant invoice. App runtime, connector, storage, orchestration and deployment costs are not all measured by this comparison.
