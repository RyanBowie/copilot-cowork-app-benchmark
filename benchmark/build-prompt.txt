REQUIRED VIEWS AND BEHAVIOR
1. Dashboard: show device model count, total available units, pending request count and total GBP value of pending requests. Values update after changes.
2. Catalogue: show device ID, name, category, unit price in GBP and available quantity. Search by device name or ID, filter by category and availability, and show a useful no-results state.
3. New request: select a device, enter positive whole-number quantity, needed-by date and nonblank business justification. Capture the current requester, department and cost centre. Show unit price and calculated total. Require a date today or later. Reject missing required values, zero/negative/fractional quantity, and quantity greater than current stock. Out-of-stock devices cannot be requested. Give clear field-level errors and a success confirmation.
4. My requests: show the current requester's requests, with request ID, device, quantity, total GBP amount, needed-by date and status.
5. Approvals: in the approver persona show pending requests with all details. Approval changes Pending to Approved and subtracts quantity from stock exactly once. Rejection requires a reason, changes Pending to Rejected and leaves stock unchanged. Only Pending requests may be acted on. Recheck stock on approval so two pending requests cannot oversell a device. Surface a clear insufficient-stock error without changing the request or stock.
6. Store changes in this app's managed persistent data store, not just in component memory, a hardcoded mock, or browser localStorage. Reloading and reopening the app must retain new requests, decisions and stock. If managed persistence is unavailable, explicitly report that limitation rather than claiming success.
7. Add a clearly labelled "Reset demo data" action with confirmation. It must reset only this app's synthetic dataset to the exact seed below, without duplicating records. Seed stock is the authoritative starting stock; do not adjust it again for historical seeded requests.
8. Use readable, professional styling, visible labels, keyboard-operable controls, clear focus states and a usable layout at desktop width and 390px mobile width. Do not use external images, packages or services unless provided by the managed-app platform itself. No unrelated extra features are needed.
